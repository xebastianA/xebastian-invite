## Brand & Style

This design system captures the gentle anticipation and tenderness of celebrating a new life. Crafted for modern baby showers, heirloom keepsakes, and registry curation, the aesthetic balances storybook nostalgia with refined editorial restraint. It eschews neon pastels and cartoonish noise in favor of a timeless, tactile, and serene atmosphere.

The style unites **Minimalist Editorial** layout principles with **Tactile / Soft-Heritage** details. Subtle paper-textured backings, pillowy elevations, and delicate hairline framing evoke bespoke stationery and artisanal letterpress. Teddy bear motifs appear as understated line illustrations, embossed accents, and warm wax-seal style tokens rather than loud novelty graphics. The emotional impression is peaceful, cherished, intimate, and deeply celebratory.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from vintage French nurseries and natural wool linens. 

- **Primary (`#768A9E` - Dusty Slate Blue):** Anchors actionable focal points, primary badges, and key interactive touchpoints. It carries quiet confidence without the clinical chill of standard tech blues.
- **Secondary (`#B8C9D9` - Soft Powder Blue):** Used for ambient fills, soft progress states, secondary chips, and delicate decorative washes.
- **Tertiary (`#D9CEBF` - Gentle Biscuit / Warm Beige):** A warm, grounding companion reminiscent of plush teddy fur and unbleached cotton ribbon, balancing the cool blue spectrum.
- **Neutral (`#2B3542` - Rich Navy Charcoal):** Serves as high-contrast typographic ink, offering substantial readability while remaining softer and more poetic than pure `#000000`.

### Supporting Surface Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` (Warm Chalk / Soft Cream White) prevents digital glare and evokes heavy matte cardstock.
- **Surface Elevated:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Cloud White) for cards, dialogs, and invitation panels.
- **Subtle Borders:** `#E3DCD2` (Warm Linen Thread) provides low-contrast separation.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy establishes a graceful contrast between historical sentiment and effortless mobile readability.

- **Display & Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys celebratory grandeur, grace, and human warmth. Display sizes benefit from natural italic variations for accents (e.g., *“Honoring Baby James”*), paired with tight tracking to feel deliberate and editorial.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Its rounded terminals and open apertures offer high legibility across RSVP forms, schedule timelines, and registry wishlists while harmonizing with the gentle curves of the design language.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Uppercase settings with generous letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) emulate traditional bookbinding foil-stamps and formal invitations.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a centered, single-to-multi column fixed-canvas philosophy inspired by stationery cards. Layouts prioritize generous breathing room around central announcement cards rather than edge-to-edge data density.

- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` edge margins. Primary invitation cards span full width minus margins to maximize intimacy and touch targets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins. Invitation cards center within a constrained maximum width of `640px`.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout with an absolute canvas constraint of `1120px`. Main invitations and RSVPs sit within a bespoke dual-column split: left panel showcasing the illustrated keepsake motif and schedule, right panel handling registry selection and interactive forms.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows a soft 4px/8px baseline rhythm, prioritizing vertical breathing space (`space-xl`) between distinct event narrative sections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh drop shadows and neon glows in favor of soft, diffused cloud shadows and tactile layering.

- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FDFBF7`. Floating invitation cards use solid `#FFFFFF` with a faint interior hairline border (`1px solid #E3DCD2`) that suggests pressed rag paper edges.
- **Ambient Shadow Character:** Shadows are wide, deeply feathered, and tinted with the neutral navy charcoal rather than pure black.
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 53, 66, 0.05), 0 1px 4px 0 rgba(43, 53, 66, 0.03)`
  - *Interactive / Lifted State:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 53, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 8px 0 rgba(43, 53, 66, 0.04)`
- **Embossed & Debossed Details:** Subdued buttons and input wells utilize internal hairline borders (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(43, 53, 66, 0.04)`) to impart a tactile letterpress feel.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a roundedness level of `2` (0.5rem base radius), producing a welcoming, nursery-safe silhouette without descending into playful cartoon geometry.

- **Base Controls (`rounded` / 8px):** Checkboxes, form fields, and compact utility buttons.
- **Container Elements (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Standard cards, event highlight callouts, countdown tiles, and guestbook submissions.
- **Focal Frames (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Hero invitation panels, modal sheets, and photo memory cards.
- **Embellishments (Fully Pill-Shaped):** Status chips, RSVP filter pills, and teddy bear stamp avatars use 9999px pill radii to echo ribbon cutouts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#768A9E` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover shifts to `#677A8D` with a subtle elevation lift. Padding: `12px 24px`. Roundedness: `9999px` (pill) for an inviting, celebratory feel.
- **Secondary / RSVP Ghost:** `#FFFFFF` background with a delicate `1px solid #B8C9D9` border and `#2B3542` text. Hover transitions background to `#F2F6F9`.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, `#768A9E` text with a subtle underline appearing only on hover; styled in `label-md` uppercase.

### Cards & Invitation Panels
- Structured with `#FFFFFF` surface fills, `16px` or `24px` corner radii, and a dual-border treatment: an outer `1px solid #E8E2D8` edge with an optional decorative inset hairline (`inset 8px`, `1px dashed #D9CEBF`) for official announcement cards.
- Teddy bear iconography or wax seal emblems sit anchored top-center across primary card headers.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields:** `#FFFFFF` backgrounds with `1px solid #D9CEBF` borders and `8px` corner radius. Focused state applies an outer halo of `0 0 0 3px rgba(184, 201, 217, 0.35)` and transitions the border to `#768A9E`.
- **Placeholder Text:** Rendered in `#8C97A4` to maintain high readability and warmth.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Soft rounded squares (`6px` radius) and circles with `1.5px solid #B8C9D9` rims.
- Checked states fill with `#768A9E` featuring a crisp white interior checkmark or center pip.

### Chips & Badges
- Registry category indicators and dietary tags use a `#F0F4F8` fill, `#2B3542` text, and a `1px solid #D5E0EB` perimeter.
- Spacing: `6px 14px`, rendered with `label-md` font settings.

### Bespoke Baby Shower Components
- **Countdown Ribbon Tile:** A horizontal ribbon component displaying Days, Hours, and Minutes set in `Playfair Display` against warm cream canvas blocks.
- **Storybook Timeline Item:** Vertical timeline for shower events (e.g., *Arrival & Mimosa Toast*, *Baby Bingo*, *Cake Cutting*) connected by a soft `1.5px` dotted slate-blue line and delicate teddy/floral node markers.
- **Gift Registry Tile:** Compact card featuring item photography, brand metadata, fulfillment status pills ("Reserved", "Available"), and a gentle direct-claim action.
